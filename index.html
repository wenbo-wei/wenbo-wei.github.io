About Me

Hello! I am Wenbo Wei (魏文博), a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Warwick. My research focuses on occlusion-aware panoptic segmentation, with the goal of enabling robust scene understanding in real-world occluded environments.

I have built large-scale occlusion benchmarks, developed plug-and-play transformer modules, and published work at IEEE ICME and ICASSP. My viva was passed in March 2026, with minor corrections expected to be completed by June 2026.

My research interests include computer vision, scene understanding, deep learning, and image segmentation.

Projects

Lightweight Occlusion-Aware Position Encoding for Robust Panoptic Segmentation 11/2024 – 06/2025

Developed a plug-and-play occlusion-aware module (PEMOLA) to boost transformer-based panoptic segmentation in occluded scenes. Achieved consistent performance gains across different occlusion severities with minimal computational overhead.

Large-Scale Occlusion Benchmark Construction and Model Optimization 06/2023 – 07/2024

Built two large-scale occlusion-annotated datasets (COCO-OLAC & Cityscapes-OLAC) with a unified manual annotation pipeline covering three occlusion levels. Systematically quantified the impact of occlusion on SOTA panoptic segmentation models and proposed a contrastive learning-based method to enhance robustness.

MRI Reconstruction based on U-Net 11/2019 – 12/2019

Proposed a U-Net-based MRI image reconstruction method for the fastMRI challenge. Achieved 1st rank in the 4-fold track and 2nd rank in the 8-fold track among 39 participating teams.
