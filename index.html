PEMOLA: Occlusion-Aware Position Encoding for Panoptic Segmentation

PhD Research

Developed a lightweight, plug-and-play module that incorporates image-dependent occlusion cues into position encoding for transformer-based panoptic segmentation. Spatial attention extracted from an occlusion classifier using Grad-CAM is combined with learnable occlusion-level embeddings to modulate position encodings along spatial and channel dimensions through residual gating.

Built Mask2Former variants with frozen SAM 3 and DINOv3 encoders to evaluate PEMOLA across backbones. With only 0.01 GFLOPs of additional computation, PEMOLA improved instance, semantic, and panoptic segmentation across COCO-OLAC and Cityscapes-OLAC.

Paper · Code · Model weights

Occlusion Benchmarks and Supervised Representation Learning

PhD Research

Established a manual annotation protocol using visible instance masks and estimated hidden extent to assign three image-level occlusion classes. Constructed COCO-OLAC (30,000 train / 5,000 val) and Cityscapes-OLAC (2,975 train / 500 val), and benchmarked panoptic segmentation methods including Mask2Former and Mask DINO.

Developed occlusion-supervised contrastive training with positive/negative feature pairing and margin constraints alongside segmentation losses, producing clearer object boundaries and better separation of adjacent instances. Designed a curriculum based on occlusion severity that used 60% fewer training updates for comparable validation performance.
