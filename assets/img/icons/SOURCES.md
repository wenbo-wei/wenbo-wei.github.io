# Publication resource icons

The arXiv, GitHub, and Hugging Face brand icons are from [Simple Icons 16.34.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.34.0), licensed under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Sources retrieved on 2026-10-06:

- [arXiv](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16.34.0/icons/arxiv.svg)
- [GitHub](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16.34.0/icons/github.svg)
- [Hugging Face](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16.34.0/icons/huggingface.svg)

The original paths are combined into a local SVG symbol sprite and use `currentColor` to follow the website's light and dark themes.

## Colored Hugging Face logo

`huggingface.svg` is the official colored Hugging Face logo, retrieved on 2026-10-06 from https://huggingface.co/front/assets/huggingface_logo-noborder.svg. It is used for the model and dataset links; the Simple Icons license above applies to the symbol sprite.
